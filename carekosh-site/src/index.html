Have another question?
We’re here to help

Who is CareKosh for?

CareKosh is for people organizing supplies and equipment for care at home, especially a home ICU setup. It helps you keep track of what you have, what’s running low, and what you need to order.

Can I use my Android account on the web?

Yes. Sign in with your existing CareKosh account to access the same inventory and orders. You don’t need a separate account for the web app.

Do I have to add every item from scratch?

You can start with the home ICU catalog of 32 items across 10 categories, or add your own items and categories. Enter the quantities you actually have and adjust minimums to suit your setup.

Does CareKosh place purchases for me?

No. Orders are lists you review, save, print, and share with your supplier. CareKosh does not buy supplies. You confirm receipt and apply quantities to your inventory separately.

What can CareKosh help with?

Ask about your current inventory, find low-stock items, or prepare an order draft. CareKosh provides inventory guidance, not clinical advice, and a draft does not change stock or place an order.

Can I export my inventory?

Yes. Download inventory as JSON, create a workspace backup, and use your browser’s Print or Save as PDF option for inventory and order reports.
