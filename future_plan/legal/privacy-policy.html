CareKosh — Privacy Policy

Draft revised 7 October 2026 — effective date to be set on publication (earlier draft dated 29 June 2026) · Operated by Rishabh Dongre · Contact: support@carekosh.com

CareKosh ("CareKosh", "we", "us") is a mobile app that helps households and home-care families track their medical and care-supply inventory — items, stock levels, expiry dates, categories, and reorder lists. This policy explains what information we collect, why, how we protect it, and the choices you have. We collect only what the app needs to work; we do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details — your name, email address, a username, and a password (used to sign in and to send verification, password-reset, and account-deletion confirmation emails). The app does not ask for your phone number.
  • Inventory you enter — item and category names, quantities, minimum-stock thresholds, expiry dates, brands, notes, supplier names/contact details (if you choose to add them), purchase links, item descriptions, and a "critical/emergency" flag; plus orders, the activity history these generate, and an internal change history (before-and-after values of item changes and order deletions) that we keep to investigate data problems.
  • Photos — if you choose to attach a photo to an item, it is selected from your device's library. The app stores the photo's on-device file reference (its URI) with the item on our server; it does not upload the photo file itself. Photos may be included in PDFs you generate.
  • Files you create — inventory/order PDFs and JSON exports you generate in the app, and inventory data you copy to your clipboard, all at your request.

Information collected automatically

  • Authentication tokens — sign-in tokens are stored securely on your device (in the operating system's secure storage) to keep you logged in.
  • Sign-in records — when you register, sign in or your session is renewed, our server records the session (an identifier, expiry and whether it has been signed out), the IP address of the request and your device's User-Agent text; the login entry in your activity history also records the IP address. These records are kept until you delete your account.
  • Limited technical / error logs — our servers keep minimal logs and error reports needed to operate, secure, and debug the service.
What we do NOT collect: no advertising identifiers, no third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app, no device location, no contacts, and no camera access (sign-in records include the IP address of the request, as described above). Microphone access is optional, Android-only and limited to the voice feature described below. Your passwords are never stored in readable form — they are hashed on our servers.

Optional AI & Voice — unpublished pilot disclosure

This optional inventory assistant is off until you enable it. Voice cannot change stock or save orders; it can prepare an unsaved local draft, which only a separate touch confirmation in Create Order saves. On Android, the new native module captures a temporary recording and can show provisional live words using the downloaded Moonshine English speech pack. Default final transcription happens on the phone after Stop; separately opted-in online listening is described below. The recording is kept only temporarily in the app's cache and is deleted after it has been converted; leftover files are removed the next time the app starts. You download the speech model explicitly in Voice setup; the download comes from Moonshine's model server, does not send recordings or inventory, and that server can receive normal connection information. If you turn on spoken replies, an offline English voice installed on your Android device reads the answer. Familiar commands are understood on your phone without any AI service. Answers are built on your phone from your own inventory data; when you are offline they use data loaded earlier in the same sign-in session and are labelled "Last known".

Optional online understanding (Groq). If you choose "Enable Groq understanding" and confirm that you are 18 or older, a question your phone cannot understand on its own is sent — only after you review it and tap Send — through our server to Groq (US) to turn the wording into a supported command. Only the question text, and whether it refers to the item shown just before, is sent; your recordings and your inventory list are not. Words and item names you include in the question are sent. Groq may retain request content for reliability or abuse monitoring unless zero data retention is enabled for our service account. This interpretation call sends no recording; separately enabled online listening can send finished audio as described below. Hosted speech and Sarvam are not offered by this mobile release. There is no automatic provider failover. With online understanding off, the assistant works on your phone; it still makes normal requests to our server to read your inventory and to check whether the online option is available. We do not upload your inventory to any AI service.

Questions, quantities and recorded suppliers can reveal health-related information. Do not include patient identifiers. You can edit the transcript before sending a question. The AI routes do not deliberately retain or log recordings, questions or spoken text. This is not a guarantee against operating-system caches, diagnostic misconfiguration or provider retention.

The online option remains disabled until the operator verifies the provider's applicable terms, data controls and this disclosure. Providers may retain operational metadata. We store, linked to your account, your assistant consent (version, the options you accepted and when) and a content-free usage record for each online request (request type, provider, time, status, an estimated cost reservation and token counts) to enforce daily limits; these records contain no question text. They are kept until you delete your account, which removes them from active storage; a shorter retention schedule must be confirmed and published before public rollout. Your assistant settings (assistant, microphone, spoken replies, online option) are stored on your device for your account. This policy update is a local release draft until published and reviewed.

Text consent alone does not authorize this upload, and old audio preferences are not restored. Your inventory list is not uploaded. Groq’s data controls also apply to this audio processing; actual account retention settings remain unverified. No question is interpreted or drafted until you review the transcript and press Send. Hosted speech and Sarvam listening remain disabled. Switch to “Use offline listening” to stop audio uploads on this device, or withdraw all cloud consent to disable both online modes and request server revocation. Local drafts do not create server orders; saving requires separate touch confirmation. The disclosure follows the current working tree; matching deployment/device checks, publication and legal review remain separate.

Withdraw online consent with "Withdraw all cloud consent" in Voice setup (Profile menu → Voice setup), or turn off the assistant, the microphone or spoken replies there separately. You can remove the downloaded speech model in the same place. A request already sent cannot necessarily be recalled; cancellation prevents late results being displayed or spoken. The online option is offered only to adults declaring they are 18 or older; this declaration is not age verification. Audience controls, provider terms and the app's broader age policy require review before public release.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To provide the core features — storing and displaying your inventory, orders, categories, and activity.
  • To send you account emails (email verification, password reset, account-deletion confirmation, and security notices such as a password change).
  • To keep the service secure, reliable, and free of abuse.
  • If you enable it, to answer questions through the optional assistant and to enforce its usage limits.

3. How your information is stored and protected

  • Your data is associated with your account and is scoped to you — other users cannot see it.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are hashed on the server (never stored in plaintext, never stored on your device).
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in device secure storage and sent to the API for authentication. The server stores refresh-session identifiers, expiry and revocation metadata, the IP address and User-Agent of each sign-in, plus account session generations.

The app also keeps a last-known inventory/order/activity display cache in device storage. It is cleared on account transitions by the app; exported or shared files are separate copies. Profile email changes are handled through support. On Android, assistant settings are stored on the device for each account, and a downloaded speech model is stored in app storage that is excluded from device backups.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and inventory data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see Section 7), we remove your personal data from our active systems; residual backup and operational-log copies require separate retention handling. The actual provider recovery windows and deletion schedule must be documented before this draft is published; this repository does not establish a verified retention period. Sign-in records, activity and change history, and assistant consent and usage records currently remain until the account is deleted; the service has no automatic time-based deletion for them.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Access and correct your information — most of it is editable directly in the app (email address changes go through support; to change a password, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen).
  • Export your inventory — the app lets you generate PDF and JSON exports.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time (Section 7).

To make any other request, email us at support@carekosh.com.

7. Account & data deletion

You can delete your account and its data in two ways:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete My Account. We send a confirmation email; deletion completes only after you open the link (valid for 24 hours) and confirm on the page it shows.
  • On the web: follow the instructions at https://carekosh.com/account-deletion.html.

Deleting your account removes your profile, inventory, orders, categories, activity history, change history, sign-in records, stored item-photo references, and assistant consent and usage records from our active systems. A per-day order-number counter, which is not linked to any account, is kept so that order numbers are never reused.

8. Children

CareKosh is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Email communications

We send only transactional emails necessary to operate your account (verification, password reset, account-deletion confirmation, and security notices). We do not send marketing email.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will revise the "Effective date" above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app or by email.

Questions or requests? Email support@carekosh.com.
