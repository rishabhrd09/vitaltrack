Delete your CareKosh account & data

App: CareKosh · Operated by Rishabh Dongre · Draft revised 7 October 2026 — effective date to be set on publication (earlier draft dated 29 June 2026)

You can permanently delete your CareKosh account and the data associated with it at any time, using either method below.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Recommended if you still have the app installed.

  1. Open CareKosh and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete My Account.
  3. We email you a confirmation link, valid for 24 hours. Your account is deleted only after you open the link and confirm on the page it shows (this protects you from accidental or unauthorised deletion). If you change your mind, do not confirm; the link expires. This option needs the email address on your account and our email service to be working — if you cannot receive the email, use Option 2.

Option 2 — Request by email (web)

Use this if you no longer have the app installed.

  1. Email support@carekosh.com from the email address registered to your CareKosh account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my CareKosh account.
  3. We will verify the request belongs to you (e.g. by confirming from your registered email) and complete the deletion. The operator must confirm the support handling process and response target before publishing this draft; no timed support workflow is established by the repository.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the following from our active systems:

DeletedDetails
Account & profileName, email, username, password hash, and authentication tokens
Sign-in recordsSession records, including the IP address and device User-Agent recorded at each sign-in
InventoryAll items, categories, quantities, expiry dates, notes, supplier details, and links
Orders & activityOrder records, your activity history, and the internal change history of your items and orders
Assistant recordsIf you used the optional online assistant: your consent record and usage records (no question text is stored)
Item photo referencesPhoto URLs stored as item metadata

What may be retained (briefly), and why

  • Encrypted backups: residual backup copies follow the configured provider retention schedule. Confirm and disclose the actual schedule before publishing; a 30-day limit has not been verified here.
  • Legal/security records: we may retain a minimal record only where required by law or to prevent abuse.
  • Order-number counter: a per-day counter (a date and the last order number used) is kept so order numbers are never reused. It contains no personal data and is not linked to your account.
  • Data on your phone: account deletion does not reach into your device. The app clears its cached inventory when it signs you out; assistant settings and a downloaded speech model stay on the phone until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app (the speech model can also be removed in Voice setup).

Files you exported yourself (PDFs, JSON) or data you copied to your clipboard live on your own device and are not controlled by us — delete those locally if you wish.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to stop using the app, you can simply uninstall it without deleting your account.
